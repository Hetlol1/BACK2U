The process

Simple. Fast. Reunited.

Four steps to get your item back — powered by AI image matching and real-time communication.

1
📝

Register Your Item

Upload a photo and description of your item. It stays private until you're ready to report it lost.

2
🔴

Mark as Lost

When something goes missing, mark it lost. It appears in the public feed for your college community.

3
✨

AI Matches Items

Our Gemini AI compares found item photos with lost items and ranks matches by confidence score.

4
🤝

Chat & Confirm

Chat with the finder directly in the app. Confirm the return and the item is marked as claimed.
